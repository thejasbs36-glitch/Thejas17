About This Portal

Committed to Student Welfare & Campus Excellence

This Complaint & Infrastructure Management Portal is a digital governance initiative to ensure every student voice is heard and every campus facility is maintained to the highest standard.

Developed under the Digital India framework, the portal connects students, teaching staff, and administration in a transparent, accountable system with defined SLAs and escalation paths.

All data is securely stored, and access is strictly role-based to maintain privacy and integrity of the grievance redressal process.
